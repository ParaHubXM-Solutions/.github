## The problem we work on

Most weather risk is still priced on low-resolution grids. A regional average is a poor
description of what actually happened to one vineyard, one solar farm, one hotel. That gap
between the measurement and the loss is basis risk, and it is the reason parametric cover
has historically underdelivered.

We close it by measuring on the ground, at the insured asset.

## How the model works

**Pricing.** Satellite reanalysis data (ERA5) is corrected by machine learning against
readings from a dense ground weather station network, so pricing reflects conditions at the
insured asset rather than a regional average. Corrections are validated against backtested
historical events.

**Settlement.** Settlement uses the station on, or nearest to, the insured asset. When the
agreed threshold is breached, the payout is calculated and released — no claim forms, no
loss adjusters.

**Zero station overhead.** Partners do not buy, install or maintain hardware. The station
network is already in place.

Every parameter — trigger, formula and data source — is agreed upfront, so cover is
transparent and auditable from day one.

## Engineering

This organization holds the platform behind the model: risk assessment and pricing,
policy lifecycle and binding, automated monitoring, and settlement.

Broadly, that is a TypeScript/Node services layer over PostgreSQL and Redis, a Python
analysis tier for weather data and actuarial modelling, and React front-ends for our
partners. The platform is multi-tenant and EU-resident, and we build to GDPR requirements
as a matter of course.

**Our repositories are private.** They carry underwriting logic, pricing models and
partner data, so they stay closed — this is deliberate, not an empty organization. If you
are a partner or carrier who needs technical detail under NDA, get in touch.
